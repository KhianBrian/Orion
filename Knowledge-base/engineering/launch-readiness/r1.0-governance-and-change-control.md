# R1.0 — Governance and Change Control

**Status:** Completed 8 September 2026 — documentation and decision preparation only.

## Purpose

Translate the 8 September 2026 owner direction into a controlled initial-launch workstream without
rewriting the Phase 0–7/D0–D7 baseline record. R1.0 produces the authority map and decision gates for
minor consent, PayMaya payments, Google Meet, in-app support tickets, the 15/45/15 session timeline,
and deferred blog/legal/support content.

## Relationship to the baseline

R1.0 consumes the original [Phase 0](../phases/phase-0-governance.md), the
[pilot decision register](../../product/pilot-decision-register.md), and the existing phase charters.
It does not reopen Phase 0 as though its historical plan had failed, and it cannot close Phase 0–6
launch gates. Its output is a superseding amendment plus future R1 work-item charters.

## Scope

- Record the owner decisions that are sufficiently concrete to guide planning.
- Identify every changed assumption in product, clinical, privacy, architecture, and engineering
  documentation.
- Preserve explicit policy gaps rather than making a technical guess.
- Define the dependency order for R1.1–R1.5.

## Not in scope

- Real personal data, payment collection, public sign-up, production Google Meet configuration, or
  real sessions.
- Final privacy, terms, telepsychiatry, parental-consent, or payment wording.
- Treating a PayMaya redirect or an unreviewed Google Meet configuration as evidence of payment or
  participant authorisation.

## Decision gates R1.0 must retain

| Topic | Required answer before implementation can be called launch-ready |
| --- | --- |
| Minors | Minimum age; guardian identity/relationship assurance; who reviews/accepts consent; parent access boundaries; clinical suitability and refusal path. |
| Payments | PayMaya merchant/API access and terms; amount/currency; reservation expiry; cancellation, refund, no-show, chargeback, receipt, and reconciliation policy. |
| Google Meet | Workspace organisation and edition; authentication and participant-admission model; host/end-session policy; vendor/privacy approval; outage procedure. |
| Support | Ticket roles; permitted ticket content; escalation, response, closure, retention, and future email ownership. |
| Notes | Clinical confirmation of the 15-minute post-session note window, early end, late note completion, manual release, and amendment handling. |
| Legal text | Approved terms, privacy, telepsychiatry, parental-consent, payment, and support wording before a real user can complete the associated flow. |

## Delivery sequence

1. Record the amendment in the decision register and establish the R1 workstream boundary.
2. Reconcile product, clinical, privacy, data-classification, appointment-lifecycle, and video-provider
   documents. A contradiction is resolved at the authority layer before a phase plan is altered.
3. Draft R1.1 and R1.2 from those reconciled authorities; they define the data and eligibility
   contracts consumed by payments and support.
4. Draft R1.3 and R1.4 only after PayMaya and Google Workspace inputs are available; do not substitute
   mock success paths for vendor-confirmed behaviour.
5. Draft R1.5 from the final ticket, payment, and provider contracts, then update Phase 6 release
   exercises and gates.

## R1.0 gate

**Completed evidence:** the decision-register amendment and R1 boundary are published; the affected
product, lifecycle, privacy, data-classification, video, RBAC, operations, and phase authorities now
either carry the R1 direction or explicitly defer to its work items; and each unresolved item above is
recorded as a blocking owner/clinical/legal/vendor decision. Completion authorises detailed planning
only; it does not authorise real-user features.
